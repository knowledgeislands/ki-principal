# Processes

## Overview

Processes are how governance is carried out in practice. Within the Model act, this chapter holds the active procedures that move knowledge from an idea to accepted content, as distinct from the conventions that describe what accepted content looks like. They are what give an island's council authority a concrete form.

The chapter has four processes. One governs change within an island; one governs how knowledge passes between a territory and the shared Knowledge Islands model; one describes how external material is acquired and harvested; one describes how coordinated work moves between the people and agents doing it. Each preserves the authority of whoever owns the receiving knowledge.

Start with [How Change Happens](<How Change Happens.md>), the narrative chapter note, before reading each process in detail.

---

## How Change Happens

[How Change Happens](<How Change Happens.md>) introduces the chapter and explains why its contents are procedures rather than policies. It distinguishes the internal process from the external one and relates both to the island's governance and council. It is the orientation to read first, before the detail of each process.

## Enactment Process

[Enactment Process](<Enactment Process/Enactment Process.md>) is the internal mechanism through which a council exercises its authority over an island's canonical content. It sets out the philosophy of the gate that change must pass before reaching Admin, Pillars or Resources, and how proposals move between Streams and ratification. The operational mechanics are owned by the shared change-management skills; this note holds the governing intent and the local specifics.

---

## Contribution Process

[Contribution Process](<Contribution Process/Contribution Process.md>) distinguishes a territory's adoption of published Knowledge Islands knowledge from a contribution to Knowledge Islands Principal's canonical model. It explains how public adoption, formal contributions, and internal routing or restricted exchange each work, and why none of them transfers authority away from the receiving owner. Read it when bringing knowledge into an island from the shared model or offering knowledge back to it.

---

## Acquisition Process

[Acquisition Process](<Acquisition Process/Acquisition Process.md>) describes the provider-neutral lifecycle by which material from AI sessions, meetings and other external sources is discovered, acquired, staged, harvested into durable knowledge and eventually retired from its transient source. It tabulates the provenance each source actually provides, records the harvest checkpoint as observed, and states that the archive and deletion threshold is still undecided. Read it when working on acquisition adapters or harvesting captured material.

---

## Work Process

[Work Process](<Work Process/Work Process.md>) is the canonical description of the work model that GDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-009 decides: territory work, regular work and Project work, and the work items beneath them; the worker and supervisor roles; the seven states, with On hold and its two reasons; the words; the work package of five files in its work folder, and Git as the logbook; routing by State of play, the inbox as a priority queue, and method stewardship with targeted refresh; the decisions life cycle; the banner; and the fields the Observatory reads. Read it when coordinating, delegating or reporting on work; the harness skills cite it rather than restate it.
