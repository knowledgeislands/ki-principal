# Timelines skill: review by Fable

**Reviewer:** Claude Fable 5.1 on Claude Code, run `tl-review-fable` · **Date:** 2026-10-19 · **Read-only:** yes

Read in this order: [the brief](timelines-skill-brief.md), [the survey](survey.md), [work.md](../work.md) and [decisions.md](../decisions.md). No other review was read. The four settled points under the Owner's proposal are taken as fixed; this review tests how the brief realises them. Case content from legal-principal is not reproduced.

## Own view

The brief is sound and the model it proposes is the right one: one dated item, three types with the span drawn in the data, busy as a separate per-item flag, firmness kept from 5G, two source shapes behind one generator, and one skill whose views are output contracts. Every Established fact checked out against its source, with two small corrections below. The weak places are at the edges, where the brief's confidence runs ahead of the evidence:

- **Spans and feeds (point 3).** The brief turns Kris's "a span shouldn't take up busy time" into "a span is never a feed event". That is stricter than the words and than the read-back in `decisions.md`, which left feed carriage for the design run, and it sits awkwardly beside point 7, which keeps 5G's precedent of writing a month-precision item as a free all-day block. I would forbid `busy` on a span and keep spans off feeds by default, with a per-set opt-in that writes them transparent.
- **Busy on a timed milestone (point 5).** A timed item with no end has zero duration under RFC 5545, so `busy: true` on it blocks nothing. The brief states the all-day rule but not this one; the rubric needs it.
- **Account sync (point 17).** The two MCP servers can write events, but neither exposes a UID-keyed update: `mcp-m365` has no update tool at all, and Microsoft Graph's `iCalUId` is read-only. Idempotent sync needs its own key mapping, a runner, deletion handling and a raised write access level. The brief's "its cost is a write-capable credential and a run" understates this.
- **The legal timeline's home (point 20).** The Command Centre Guide already forbids a second live artifact and the existing one already has a Calendar view. In legal the generated timeline is a view of that artifact or a generated Working Note, not a new live artifact pair.
- **Adoption (points 26-27).** The order is right. The pilot should be vendoring plus parity alone, with 5G's timeline collapse as its own record, and legal's adoption should split into a local half (source, times, generated timeline) and a sync half, so the time-critical value does not wait on the credential decision.

On the name, `ki-key-dates` is defensible and gains an argument the brief missed: `ki-calendars` would also collide with the `Calendar/` zone that every base already has for periodic notes. The name remains the owner's call.
