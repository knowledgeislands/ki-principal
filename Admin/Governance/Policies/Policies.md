# Policies

## Overview

Policies hold persistent operating constraints under Knowledge Islands Principal's governance. Substantive policy changes follow the [[Admin/Operations/Processes/Enactment Process|Enactment Process]]; a policy's presence or successful audit does not replace its owner approval.

## Techne Programme Focus

The [[Techne Programme Focus]] narrows Techné's two implementation products to building and running the agent host `vega` and the Mac Studio `sol`, under operating bounds carried forward from the former Techne Programme Hold. Every other piece of Techné work is kept as an idea until Kris takes it up through the Enactment Process. Both products stay building and current with their repository standards, and ordinary CI continues.
