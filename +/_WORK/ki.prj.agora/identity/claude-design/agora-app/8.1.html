8.1 · Guilds (superseded by 8.2)

The guilds and their skills, read from each *-agentic-harness island on the lands, and a chosen skill's documents (src/app/guilds.ts, skills.ts). Kris asked for "each of the guilds ... some kind of panel with a logo on it, what the guild is about, and then underneath it a bank of skills". It matters because seventy-odd skills are only findable today by knowing their folder; the badges make the guilds a place to browse, and the pane reads a skill without leaving Atlas. The territory is the context control's. Until the guildmaster's catalogue (ki.reg.guilds) names the guilds, each harness folder stands in, and the page says so.

Refreshed 2026-11-06 · source: mock-ups from build/plan.py; not yet built

What it shows
  • Guild badges (P2), across: All guilds, then each guild's badge: its logo or monogram on its own colour, its name and how many skills; a badge is a tally, so choosing it keeps that guild
  • Skills (P3): the chosen guild's about line and its skills as sub-badges, each with its guild's small shield; with All guilds, a ledger, one guild a row
  • Words to find a skill by name, description or guild
  • Bottom pane (B1), a skill chosen: its guild and harness, its name, and a tab for SKILL.md (front matter, then its instructions drawn), each other document and each folder (references/, scripts/, assets/) with its files
  • Notices: provisional guilds; a harness without skills; another territory's own unsorted skills as one guild

Region codes: B, the bottom pane (green); P, page content (brass). Quote them as "8.1a P2".
