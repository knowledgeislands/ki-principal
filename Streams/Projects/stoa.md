# Stoa

## Outcome

Stoa is the shared site package kept in [`packages-stoa`](https://github.com/knowledgeislands/packages-stoa): base styles and tokens, the Header, the Page Foot, the Theme Engine and Theme Menu, the Calendar, the Command Bar, the Filter Bar, the Diagram Viewer and the other units sites vendor whole. This Project takes the asks sites put to Stoa, shapes them into Stoa's records and releases, and keeps Stoa free of any reference to the territories and sites that use it.

Kris chose to split Humans Not Robots' portal-framework work on 31 October 2026, the Stoa part moving to Knowledge Islands, and confirmed hnr.prj.portal-framework concluded on 2 November (PORTAL-CLOSE). He took STOA-PROJECT a) on 7 November 2026: "Yeah, definitely go ahead on those. I thought we were already doing it." The Project is registered on hold until Kris opens it.

This Project sits in [Foundations](../Themes/foundations.md).

---

## Notes

- **No consumers.** Kris on 1 November: "There should be no reference out of Knowledge Islands to other territories." Where Stoa needs to know its consumers, it discovers them in the local estate rather than keeping a list.
- **Sites.** Each site keeps its own content and its own adoption of Stoa; this Project owns only the package.
