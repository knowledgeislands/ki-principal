3.2 · Broadcasts

What Kris said to everyone, and who heard it. It matters because a broadcast such as Away opens an unattended window: Kris needs to know every agent took it.

Refreshed 2026-11-06 · source: mock-ups from build/plan.py; not yet built

What it shows
  • Sent
  • What
  • Kind: message, away, back, ping, mark
  • Heard: answered of reached
  • Silent: who did not answer
  • For the selected one: each agent, answered or silent
