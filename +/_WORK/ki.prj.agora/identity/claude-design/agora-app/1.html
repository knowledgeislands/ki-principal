1 · Home

One view that fits the window with no scrolling (MSG-62774774, Kris's vision): the territories and their islands, the work packages and workers placed where they work, the primary focus of each, the island portal on each island, the threads of talk up to each territory's principal portal and across, and the planar portal, on its own island ki-portal, between the planes. The preferred home (1f) makes it a working map: active islands stand out, Focused hides the quiet ones, each island carries its queued work and waiting messages, and clicking an island shows its detail in the right tray. It matters because it is the page the app opens on, and it should show the whole distributed Agora as one place where Kris can see where work is and pick it up.

Refreshed 2026-11-06 · source: mock-ups from build/plan.py; not yet built

What it shows
  • Toolbar: Focused or Everything; the plane (sol, terra or both); zoom out, the level, zoom in, Fit; Show as a list
  • Territories, each a faint boundary with its name
  • Islands (repositories), drawn as hexes: active as land, quiet as outlines
  • On each island: its name, queued work (a stack) and waiting messages (an envelope), each a small count; zero left out
  • Island portal on each island: lit when someone there listens; the principal portal larger on each principal island
  • The planar portal on ki-portal in Personal, ringed in gold, kept by the watchman; sol and terra, open or not
  • Workers by role: council, enabler, doer; each with its primary focus and rig
  • Listening by shape: solid listening, hollow on open, slashed silent
  • Threads of talk: island portals up to the principal portal, principal portals across, the planar portal out to the other plane
  • Right tray, an island selected: where it is, its portal, checkouts, who works there, queued work, waiting messages, what waits on Kris
  • Right tray, nothing selected: at a glance and the key
  • Zoomed: labels stay at reading size and add when each worker last answered; an overview shows where the view sits

Region codes: P, page content (brass). Quote them as "1f P2".
