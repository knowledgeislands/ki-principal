# Agora identity studies

The meeting ring anchors the landscape, the slide vignettes and the icons. Sage terrain and warm limestone sit against slate-teal water. Amber marks an action in the slide illustrations.

## Places

- [Causeway](concept-causeway.png) - An open meeting ground above the sea, with citizens walking over from the smaller island.
- [Harbour](concept-harbour.png) - The arrival begins at the waterline. A boat lands below the ring, with a footbridge to the second island.
- [Tidal crossing](concept-tidal-crossing.png) - An overhead view of the stone court and the stepping slabs that connect the two shores.

## Icons

| State | SVG | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| All well | [Meeting ring](menu-all-well.svg) | Six solid stones around an open centre |
| A Mac has gone quiet | [Hollow stone](menu-mac-quiet.svg) | The upper-right stone becomes an outline |
| Something needs a look | [Diamond](menu-needs-a-look.svg) | A diamond sits inside the ring |
| Unread news | [Dot](menu-unread-news.svg) | A smaller round dot sits inside the ring |

The menu-bar PNGs have black artwork on transparent backgrounds for use as macOS template images. The filename suffix gives the pixel size. Use 16 and 22 for the two target sizes, or 32 and 44 for their corresponding 2x assets.

The [full-colour app icon](agora-app-icon.png) sets the same ring on a low hexagonal island, with a smaller island across the causeway. The PNG is 1024 × 1024. The [SVG master](agora-app-icon.svg) is editable.

## Slide illustrations

- [Agents showing up](slide-arrival.png) - A small procession steps ashore and heads for the circle.
- [A call to an agent](slide-call.png) - A single amber arc links two citizens.
- [A broadcast to everyone](slide-broadcast.png) - One speaker sends a signal out to the listeners around the circle.
- [Two Macs kept in step](slide-in-step.png) - Two equal islands with matching meeting grounds and a shared crossing.
- [News and the record](slide-record.png) - One citizen announces the news while another writes it in an open ledger.

The landscapes and vignettes were generated individually with built-in ImageGen. Each prompt called for a standalone landscape in architectural ink and matte gouache, with hexagonal terrain, limestone, muted sage, slate-teal water and soft light. The scene descriptions above give the individual prompts' subjects. The slide prompts also specified simple forms and pale blue-grey space around the action. The icons were drawn as vectors.
