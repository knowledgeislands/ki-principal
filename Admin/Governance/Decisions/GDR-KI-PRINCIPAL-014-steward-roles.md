# GDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-014: Steward roles

## Context

[[SDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-006-agents-in-the-knowledge-islands-model|SDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-006]] names the types of agent (human, artificial tool and agentic AI) and their standing (Visitor, Citizen, Council). It names no roles. The `ki-design` work drew workers on the map by role - Steward, Archivist, Cartographer, Builder, Navigator, Scout, Historian and Guardian - and asked whether to write them into the Agents model or map them onto what it already describes, before a public Field Guide shows them. The earlier explainer boards used other names: Curator, Gardener, Harbourman, Observer and Governance.

On 2026-10-19 Kris accepted the recommendation to map the roles first and add only what is missing. Under [[SDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-004-the-enactment-process|SDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-004]] his explicit instruction for this bounded change is the ratification.

## Decision

**A role is a hat, not a species.** Any agent, human or AI, may wear one. A role names work the model already describes; it grants no authority. What an agent may do still follows from its standing and the island's process.

**Seven roles are official:** Steward, Archivist, Cartographer, Builder, Navigator, Scout and Historian. Each maps onto work the model already describes, set out in [[Pillars/Philosophy/Model/Agents/Agents|Agents]].

**The Guardian is not adopted as a separate role.** Holding an island's boundaries and authority is the Council's work, done in the Council Hall and checked by Conformance; the Steward wears that hat. A shield-bearer would also sit awkwardly with the non-combative principle of [[GDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-011-the-capital|GDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-011]].

**Two limits carry over from the agent model.** Ratification is a Council act, so an AI agent wearing the Steward's hat tends but does not ratify. Reflection is the distinctly human stage of the cycle, so an AI agent wearing the Historian's hat prepares the retrospect and a person judges it.

## Consequences

- The Agents chapter carries the role mapping.
- `ki-design` and the KI Website mark the seven roles official and drop the Guardian, drawing boundary-keeping as the Steward at the Council Hall.
- The explainer boards' names read as these roles: Curator as the Archivist, Gardener as the Steward, Harbourman as the Navigator, Observer as the Historian, and Governance as the Steward.
