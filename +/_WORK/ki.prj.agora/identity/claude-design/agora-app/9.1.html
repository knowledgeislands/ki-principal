9.1 · Home: who holds what

Home, reached from the Agora Atlas logo (0.4). Kris's review asks for "who holds what": one row per live package with its role, tier, rig, focus areas and state, with a footnote wherever a package's record disagrees with the guild catalogue, and the roles the catalogue names that nobody holds yet. Needs a look sits above it, each item saying what it is and what to do, so the page Atlas opens on answers "is anything wrong, and who is on it?" first. It matters because the map shows where work is; Home shows who answers for it.

Refreshed 2026-11-06 · source: mock-ups from build/plan.py; not yet built

What it shows
  • Needs a look (P2): urgent or look, each a mark and a word; what it is; what to do; one plain action
  • Who holds what (P3): Role (Kris's emoji and its word), Tier (Leader, Manager, Doer), Package (the work id), Rig, Focus areas on one line (⭐ and bold for the primary, ⏰ for something scheduled), State (🤖 Working, 👤 With you, 💤 Idle, 🔕 Silent)
  • Doers appear as Doer rows, with the package that brought them in
  • Footnotes (P4): † ‡ § mark the cell where the record and the catalogue disagree; the row shows the catalogue
  • Yours to approve (P5): catalogue roles not yet created, with the proposed package and rig; the one brass action approves the section
  • Right tray (T1), a row chosen: its facts, its focus areas with their schedule, and the record beside the catalogue

Region codes: P, page content (brass); T, the tray and pop-outs (blue). Quote them as "9.1a P2".
