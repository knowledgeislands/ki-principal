- 2026-11-01 · FOOTER-CAPABILITY-STATUS-2 · Personal > Rig > terra (personal.reg.rig-terra): Kris's refinement of FOOTER-CAPABILITY-STATUS, replacing the suggested wording. Each thread carries four fields: `listening_enabled: true/false`, `listening_status: on/off`, `syncing_enabled: true/false`, `syncing_status: on/off`. Cross-field rule: if `syncing_enabled` is true, `listening_enabled` must be true, because a syncing thread is always a listening thread. The footer shows a capability's line only when its `_enabled` is true, and the line shows its status. For listening that is `👂 listening` when on and `🔇 not listening` when off; syncing needs the matching pair (for example `🔄 syncing` and a "not syncing" form). With `_enabled` false there is no line.
