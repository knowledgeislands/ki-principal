0.4 · Atlas shell, revised

Kris's review (22:55 to 23:20 GMT) of the 0.3 sidebar. Home leaves the sidebar and is reached by clicking the Agora Atlas logo, as on a website; the map that was Home becomes Known Lands, under Work, with an old chart for its glyph. Every place now sits in a section with a smaller eyebrow, every section folds, and folded to icons each section keeps a three-letter eyebrow: AGO, WRK, GLD, OBS, KIV, SYS. Accounts, Settings and Updates form SYS at the foot, above the portal line. It matters because the sidebar is on every page: one rule for every section is quicker to learn than groups, a lone place and a foot that each behaved differently. Every page in the pack now draws this sidebar, except 0.1's and 0.3's own boards.

Refreshed 2026-11-06 · source: mock-ups from build/plan.py; not yet built

What it shows
  • Logo (N5): Agora Atlas in the toolbar is Home; it is underlined while Home is open, and says "Home" on hover
  • Sections (N2): AGO Agora, WRK Work (Known Lands, Roadmap, Jobs, Art gallery later), GLD Guilds, OBS Observatory, KIV (folded while it is coming)
  • Eyebrow: 9.5-point capitals with a fold chevron; folded, it carries its count of places and, in a gold-edged word, what inside wants a look
  • Folded to icons (⌘\): the three-letter eyebrow heads each section; a folded section is a dashed box with its letters and a chevron
  • Foot (N3): SYS (Accounts, Settings, Updates), then the portal between the rigs, Beacon and Lantern
  • Known Lands: a curled chart with a dotted path to an X, so it reads as a map and not as a graph

Region codes: C, the context control (slate); N, navigation (teal); P, page content (brass); T, the tray (blue). Quote them as "0.4a P2".
