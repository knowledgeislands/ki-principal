# Retro 2026-10-20 - ki.baseline-rollout

## Working well

- Blind reviews changed two recommendations before they reached Kris: VA-PLANNING was split by lifecycle state, and THEME-REGISTRY was widened to all six Capitals.
- Kris's "Anything high confidence can be done." let eight queries move in one answer. Within about 20 minutes, five raise runs had captured triage records in ten repositories, each committed locally and passing audit.

## Not working

- The waiter is a shell loop tied to the session, so it dies when the session does. wave1-review sat unread from 19:43 to 23:27. Tonight's three reviews finished at 00:35 and were not read until 02:04, so the overnight autonomy Kris granted did nothing for 90 minutes.
- The reviews were not blind. Each reviewer read `queries.md`, which shows the recommendation and its confidence, and all three recorded this as a limitation.
- Medium-confidence wording choices piled up. Fifteen queries were open at the hold, and most were titles or theme labels on records that stay in triage until adopted.
- `method_read` still holds a UTC time rather than the harness revision (METHOD-READ), carried since 18:03 on 19 October.
- Untracked `*.report.md` mirrors of every run's report appeared in the work folder overnight. They sit outside the package's closed list, and nothing in this work wrote them.

## Would change

- Run the waiter as a detached `ki agent` watcher or a scheduled wake, so that a lost session no longer stops the overnight work.
- Give reviewers a masked copy of each query in the brief, Context and Query only, and keep `queries.md` out of their reading list.
- Let wording-only queries on triage records take the recommended option at medium unless Kris objects, because adoption is the real checkpoint.
