# Coordination for any work package: brief

**Owner:** Kris Brown. **Owning work:** `ki.ways-of-working`. **Owning repository:** knowledgeislands/ki-principal. **Date:** 2026-10-28. **Status:** Design only.

## Problem

Regular work may hold a package but is expressly barred from coordinating sessions by the current method. Acquire has a finite dispatch backlog inside ongoing regular work. Its approved interim coordination exception must operate while the general eligibility, home, pointer and tending rules are considered separately. A backlog does not by itself require a new permanent coordinator or Project identity.

## Owner's proposal

Please take COORD-ANY-PACKAGE from your messages.md now, ahead of the queue. It's from Personal > Acquire intake (personal.regular.acquire-intake).

My proposal: any work that has a work package can have a coordination thread. That includes regular work (`regular`) just like Project work (`project`). State of play is a specialisation of it, coordinating several work packages through its own special work package (`sop`). Conversational threads stay for one-off work such as individual captures.

This revises REGULAR-WORK-ROLE from 23 October, which told Acquire intake not to coordinate. The trigger: Acquire has a finite backlog that needs coordinating, and its regular package is the natural home for it. That backlog is a context refresh of eight territories, eight captures awaiting a territory, about 385 captures to dispatch, and four triage records.

Please weigh it alongside the related lines from the same sender: PKG-HOME, PKG-POINTER and TEND-PATTERN. Also weigh HNR's METHOD point that packages should live in the principal by default.

Come back with a recommendation and what it changes: the levels table, the bootstrap check that today stops on `regular`, the hook and the output style. Until you decide, I'm treating Acquire intake as an approved exception.

The 28 October 15:05 GMT owner entry approves recommendation work and Acquire's interim exception only. Sender workload counts above are reported context, not an independently verified inventory. No general adoption, package move, hold release or live identity change is authorised.

## Reflection

Review protocol: first read the problem, proposal, facts and underlying sources and write your own recommendation. Only then read the numbered claims below. Do not read other reviews or governing package decisions, queries or reports. Each claim is a recommendation for owner consideration, not an enacted rule.

1. Make every valid package eligible for an explicitly opened coordinating session, while package existence and regular/project/sop identity do not automatically select that role. Keep conversational one-off capture and bounded direct work available. No stored role field is needed to express this policy.
2. Treat SoP as specialised coordination across assigned packages, retaining territory authority, parent/covers and per-package state/holds. Preserve responsive-coordinator delegation rules whenever a session explicitly coordinates, including regular work.
3. Coordinate Acquire's finite backlog in its existing regular package under the approved exception. End that coordinating episode when the backlog is received or handed back; ongoing regular work does not imply a mandatory permanent coordinating session. Do not reopen historical acquisition runs or broaden provider authority.
4. Recommend principal-default homes with explicit owner-approved member exceptions declared in Scope. Acquire remains at its actual member home. Home selection does not move records, acceptance or execution sovereignty, nor prove where sessions run.
5. Prefer a discoverable principal reference to the one real package over PKG-POINTER's second live package. Use existing governed discovery/routing surfaces and a plain reference, with no invented live schema, rather than missing/fake state files under +/_WORK/. The exact pointer representation remains separately decidable.
6. Preserve one authoritative inbox at the real home. A principal relay, if desired, is an explicit route through the existing SoP queue, not another package inbox. It requires a stable origin reference, idempotent receipt, destination commit before source removal, loop rejection, and conflict-safe multi-host retry; no promise of exactly-once transport or conflict elimination is justified. Prefer direct authorised messaging where this relay machinery is unnecessary.
7. Require a named package steward, principal discovery of owner-approved member exceptions and regular/ad hoc work, and review of coverage gaps at package opening/move and ordinary tending. The receiver owns findings/disposition and package tidy; SoP surfaces missing ownership and stale/unreachable homes without silently assuming foreign stewardship. Neither automatic execution nor a new scheduler is implied.
8. Enact only after owner choices and source safety: reconcile the MOD011 candidate, amend canonical model and harness sources coherently, regenerate marked runtime surfaces once, update actual CLI/status/placement consumers through their owners, then pilot without live renames until compatible readers and territory-owner gates pass. Use focused role/home/message-retry regressions rather than broad builds or audits for this design.
