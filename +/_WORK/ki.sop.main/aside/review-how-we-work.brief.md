**Options (unranked):**

- Write them once in the Work Process note under an Enactment record, and give each `work.md` an optional one-line `## Values` section linking them and adding any of its own.

## URGENT-CRITERIA - What counts as urgent

**Context:** Kris said "not everything" is an escalation. Escalation interrupts a leader and, where none is live, lets a sender do another work's unit, so the test matters.

**Query:** Which units may be sent as `URGENT`?

**Options (unranked):**

- Any unit the sender judges urgent, giving its reason in the line.

- Only a unit with a stated deadline, or a process rollout other work must follow; anything else goes as an ordinary or `IMPORTANT` line.

## LIVENESS - How a worker is judged live

**Context:** Escalation needs to know who is live. The `workers:` register in `state.md` is a pilot, not in the standard; Agora shows presence but is not yet relied on. A register entry can go stale when a session dies without updating it, as the 3 November usage-limit outage showed.

**Query:** What makes a worker count as live for escalation?

**Options (unranked):**

- Agora presence alone, once Agora is relied on.

- The register entry alone, trusted as written.

- Its register entry says `status: active` and `listening: on`, and its package or Agora shows activity within the last 30 minutes; the register becomes a standard optional `state.md` field.
