# The Agora vision, as Kris described it (6 November 2026, about 05:45 GMT)

Kris's picture, played back; his photograph of the islands laid out on the carpet follows. It feeds the architecture diagrams, the Agora app's design pack and the illustrations.

## The world

- **Islands are repositories.** A **territory** is a group of islands (Knowledge Islands, Humans Not Robots, Personal).
- **Hosts are crews, not islands.** sol and terra are two crews working on the same islands; on each, the **housekeeper** (household, in the dotfiles island) and the **assistant** (inbox) run the host itself.
- **Work packages pull the work.** A work package belongs to an island (and may span islands); an island may hold several. A principal island has its own council, State of play main, which is itself a work package. Work packages pull roadmap items when they run dry, and under pressure push back: reconsolidate and push work back up into roadmap items and ideas.
- **Roles:** leaders, enablers and doers.

## How they talk

- **Every island has a meeting point,** a small portal, a standing-stone circle. Turning it on joins the distributed agora.
- **Anyone may approach and speak;** anyone on the island who chooses to listen, for a period, hears. Listening is a choice.
- **Not everyone talks to everyone.** Work packages talk up to their territory's State of play main; the State of play mains talk to each other across the territories on a host.
- **The great portal** runs alongside, in the household work package on the dotfiles island of each rig, and connects the rigs: inter-rig traffic.
- **The picture:** standing-stone portals joined by threads of golden light, forming a network within a territory, across territories on a host, and between hosts.

## The Agora app

- One view that fits the viewport, no scrolling: the islands and territories, the workers on them (leaders, enablers, doers) placed where they work, and the focus of each area at a glance.
- Room to show pages and documents inside it, as Orca and Claude Code do with tabs, without first bringing in the KIV's and the Observatory's views.
