Standards: background runs

A background run is detached: no one watches it as it works, so its packet must say everything it may do. Each run is launched through ki agent, recorded in the run queue, and ends with an authority footer and a report.

Its rules come from assets/rules-none.md to assets/rules-release.md, one per grant.
