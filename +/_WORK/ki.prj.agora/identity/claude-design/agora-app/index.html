Agora app · design pack

The rich Electron app beside the tray (DESIGN-PACK, Kris about 05:40 GMT). It first shows what is happening within each Agora and across them; later it takes in the KIV's inbox and the Observatory's insight. One page per part, in the app's own order: the shell and tray first, then home, then each view. Where a page has alternatives, they are lettered and the preferred one is outlined in brass with its reason.
