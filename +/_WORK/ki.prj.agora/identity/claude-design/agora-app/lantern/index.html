Agora Lantern · Who is listening

The iPhone companion to the Agora app, with one screen. Kris picks it up, one-handed, to see at a glance which Macs are present and which agents are listening, and to ping everyone if he doubts it. It only shows: it grants no authority and changes nothing in Git. Each artboard is 393 by 852 points, with the status bar, Dynamic Island and home indicator drawn, in night (the reference) and in light. Three groupings are offered; the preferred one is outlined in brass with its reason.

Refreshed 2026-11-06 · source: lantern/build.py · tokens from the pack's app.css

What it shows
  • Summary line: "12 agents on 2 Macs · 9 listening", then a key with a count for each state.
  • Each Mac (sol, terra): present, or unknown when it cannot be reached, with the time last heard.
  • Each agent: package id, territory, focus areas with the primary starred, and listening / on open / silent.
  • How many queries wait on Kris, when any do. Tapping a row opens its detail.
  • The roster is the pack's: 12 agents, sol 5 and terra 7, including the three watchmen. Illustrative data, 6 November 2026.

Quote a region as "a L7" or "a-detail L10". The same code means the same thing on every artboard; a box marks the first example on a screen.
