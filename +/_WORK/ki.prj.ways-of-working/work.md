# ki.prj.ways-of-working

## Goals

- Deliver the [Ways of working](/Users/krisbrown/kis/knowledge-islands/ki-principal/Streams/Projects/ways-of-working.md) Project's outcome.
- While the method is being defined, send refresh lines straight to live works; afterwards method stewardship passes to State of play (WOW-REFRESH, STEWARD-HOME).

## Scope

- In: the Work Process model, the work package and its migration, `ki-coordination`, `ki-delegation`, the coordinator output style, work-record fields and their migration, method changes routed by State of play.
- Out: territory dispatch and the Project queue (ki.sop.main); `ki work status` and tools-ki (ki.prj.tools-burndown); the Observatory (ki.prj.observatory); the `work`, `knowledge` and `acquire` specifications (ki.prj.portable-specifications); Rig and dotfiles (ki.prj.chezmoi, ki.prj.mac-studio-setup).

## Focus

1. `package-layout` - the work package's shape: its five files, the focus-folder layout, tidy and close-out.
2. `discussions` - the discussion file and its readers.
3. `host-sync` - host synchronisation and request/reply trades.
4. `host-reconcile` - reconciling diverged checkouts: resolve runs and the rebase rule.
5. `inbox-wake` - the inbox watcher and waking a thread from outside.
6. `orca-subagents` - Orca's fit with the work model, its adapter and release.
7. `capture-model` - acquisition and capture: the assistant skill, its store and held lines.
8. `empty-board` - an empty owner board and the work-in-progress limit.
9. `window-31-oct` - unattended windows: the AWAY-31-OCT plan and interim policy cover.
10. `landscape` - where Knowledge Islands sits among the tools.
11. `work-model` - the work state machine, typed ids, the model shape and tags.
12. `rollout` - rolling the method out: conform sweeps, burn-down and field migration.
13. `personal-shape` - the Personal territory's shape and its handover.
14. `aside` - anything outside the others.
