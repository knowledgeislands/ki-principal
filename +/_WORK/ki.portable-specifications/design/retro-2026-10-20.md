# Retro - ki.portable-specifications, 20 October 2026

## Working well

- Blind Codex reviews found real defects every round: draft-review-3 caught an overstated validator claim, draft-review-5 caught that the same claim missed silent truncation, and query-review-6 found HOOK-GUARD already superseded and that parallel runs need separate worktrees.
- Chaining each revise run to its review with `--wait-for` kept the work moving overnight: drafts 0.4 to 0.8 and five reviews ran between 00:07 and 00:59 with no idle gap.
- The high-confidence threshold for overnight authority held: LINK-FIELDS, CATALOGUE-WRITE and HOMING-REVIEW were taken only after a review lifted them, and SPEC-TEXT was kept for Kris.

## Not working

- `git commit -- <paths>` with untracked files failed silently behind an error filter, so the mark and live-read commits did not land until a recheck at 23:52.
- The shared index with other sessions caused lock waits at 00:40 and let other sessions' commits sweep in staged moves, and the unpushed stack in ki-principal reached 62 commits, only 11 of them this work's.
- The review loop converged slowly: the validator wording was corrected in three successive drafts, because each revise fixed one instance rather than the class.
- Kris's earlier "skill extension" framing was not in any work file when he cited it, so KNOWLEDGE-AS-SKILL had to be recorded from his restatement; and two decision timestamps were written wrong and later fixed.

## Would change

- Brief each revise to fix the class of a finding across all files, and stop the review loop when no must-fix remains, carrying should-fix items to the owner's read.
- Commit through a helper that adds untracked paths first and checks `git log -1` and status afterwards, and take decision timestamps only from `date`.
