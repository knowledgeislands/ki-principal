7.1 · Roadmap

Every work record on the lands this Mac carries, read from each island's docs/roadmap/ or a knowledge base's Streams/Roadmap/ front matter, as the built app reads it (src/app/roadmap.ts). Kris asked for "the roadmap table ... filter it based on the summary table ... and on different factors", with the details in the right-hand tray. It matters because the records are spread over a dozen islands: this is the one place to see what is in progress, what waits for review and what is piling up in triage, and to pick one up. It settles the summary-filter bar (7.1.1) that Jobs shares.

Refreshed 2026-11-06 · source: mock-ups from build/plan.py; not yet built

What it shows
  • Tallies (P2): All open, then each open status (in progress, awaiting review, ready, draft, triage) with its count; a click keeps only that status, ⌘-click adds one; done and cancelled are one tally, hidden until chosen
  • Chips (P2): the territory from the context first, then each chosen value, removable; one Filter menu for priority, horizon, area, size, island, kind and assignee, each value with its count
  • Words to find in an id or title; Group by status, island, area or none
  • Records (P3): id, title, island, status (mark and word), priority and size, when it last changed; a long group folds after a few rows
  • Right tray (T1), a record chosen: its front matter, what it is blocked by and blocks, its Goal and Context; Open the file, Read in full (the bottom pane), Show on Home

Region codes: P, page content (brass); T, the tray and pop-outs (blue). Quote them as "7.1a P2".
