# Lore Sheet

## Overview

The Lore Sheet names the motifs that give Knowledge Islands its world: the recurring images that stories, illustrations and copy may draw on. Each motif stands for something the model already means, so the world stays a map of meaning rather than decoration. Where a motif is also a symbol, the [Symbol Library](<../Symbol Library/Symbol Library.md>) holds its official meaning. [GDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-015](../../../Admin/Governance/Decisions/GDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-015-the-lore-sheet.md) adopted this page.

---

## Motifs

**The Great Library.** Canonical knowledge, kept with care. Arcadia's Great Library is a reference other islands may consult, never a jurisdiction over them; the libraries the world has lost are the reason it is tended.

**The Capital.** The civic heart of an island, holding the Library and the Council Hall. It is a square and a forum, open and welcoming, and never fortified.

**The Port and its customs house.** Where material enters and leaves an island, by dock, rail depot or airport. At the customs house what crosses is inspected and declared; nothing passes from the Port straight into the Library.

**The Mill.** The running of the island: its routines and upkeep, turning steadily like a waterwheel. It stands for `Admin/Operations`.

**The tides.** The cycle of knowledge - capture, connect, reflect - coming round at many cadences. Motion on the map is slow and tidal, never hurried.

**The Known Lands and their fog.** The charted inventory of a territory and the lands it knows about. Fog lies where the chart is thin: experimental ideas, unvisited lands and knowledge not yet settled. It lifts as islands are visited and charted.

**The compass.** Orientation across the Realm. It may serve as a small orientation mark, but it never stands in for the logo.

**The Ruins.** Honoured retirement. What an island or a body of knowledge has retired is kept as ruins - archived, read-only and visited for what it teaches - rather than erased. Weathering towards ruin shows age and neglect, never failure or blame.

## Ruled out

Knowledge Islands draws no **creatures**, no **magic** and no **crystals**. The world is built by people and their work: no sea monsters or dragons, no spells or glowing runes, no crystals of power.

**Whale song**, proposed as a signal, is a creature and is ruled out with them unless Kris decides otherwise. Signals are carried by built things, such as the Lighthouse.

The world stays non-combative, as set out in [Visual Style](<../Visual Style/Visual Style.md>).
