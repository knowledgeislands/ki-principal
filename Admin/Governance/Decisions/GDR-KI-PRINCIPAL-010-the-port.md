# GDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-010: The Port

## Context

[[SDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-002-the-home-of-knowledge|SDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-002]] made the Harbour the entry metaphor for the inbound working area `+/`, with `-/` as the outbound working area. A harbour only covers arrival by sea, but material reaches an island in more ways than that: carried in by hand, on regular schedules, and from distant systems and agents. Leaving is as much a part of the boundary as arriving.

The Symbol Library also carried the Port and the Harbour as two separate symbols, the Port meaning "operational systems" and the Harbour meaning "integration point". Two harbour-like places with unrelated meanings confused readers and drawings alike. The same library gave the Lighthouse a double meaning, "guidance / governance", while the Council Hall, where governance actually happens, had no symbol of its own.

On 2026-10-18 Kris instructed that the Port replaces the Harbour as the island's point of entry and exit, because a port has more modes than a harbour. He had earlier instructed that the Lighthouse means guidance and the Council Hall means governance. Under [[SDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-004-the-enactment-process|SDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-004]] his explicit instruction for this bounded change is the ratification.

## Decision

**The Port is the island's point of entry and exit.** The inbound working area `+/` and the outbound working area `-/` are the Port's two sides: material arriving for triage, and material prepared to leave. Staging in the Port is not canonical knowledge and grants neither acceptance nor permission to transfer. Nothing flows directly from the Port into the Library.

A Port has four modes, each a district of one Port:

- **Dock** - by sea; exchange with other islands, and material carried in by hand.
- **Rail depot** - scheduled, high-volume flows along fixed lines.
- **Airport** - fast, long-range connections to distant lands: other systems and agents.
- **Customs house** (or border crossing) - where what enters or leaves is inspected and declared.

The Port absorbs the Harbour's meaning of "integration point". The Harbour is retired as a Knowledge Islands term and symbol; "harbour" survives only as plain English and as the Port's Arcadian register in prose. The former Port meaning of "operational systems" moves to `Admin/Operations`, whose drawn form is not yet decided.

**The Lighthouse means guidance. The Council Hall means governance:** decisions, not storage.

## Consequences

- The Symbol Library, Visual Style, Structure, The Home of Knowledge and the Philosophy notes that name the island's places use the Port, and the Lighthouse and Council Hall with their separate meanings.
- Earlier Decision Records and roadmap records keep their Harbour wording as a historical record; read "Harbour" in them as the Port under this record.
- The KI Website and `ki-design` switch their Harbour wording to the Port and mark the Port, its four modes, the Lighthouse and the Council Hall as canon.
- How `Admin/Operations` is drawn stays open in `ki-design` until a later decision.
