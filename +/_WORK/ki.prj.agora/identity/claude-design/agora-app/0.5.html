0.5 · Atlas bar, compact

Kris's review (23:05 GMT) of the toolbar: it took too much room, the sol and terra chips would not hold ten rigs, counts such as "2 must settle" said nothing on hover, and At a glance was unclear and popped out. The bar is now one 36-point row. The rigs are one presence control, its mark the worst state among them, opening a list of every rig; they show as chips only while three or fewer fit. The counts are one notification indicator, its count and worst state as a mark and a word, leading to Home's Needs a look (9.1), where each item says what it is and what to do. At a glance is gone: its contents are on Home. Every control has a tooltip in words. It also settles one rule for the right tray and every pop-out (IA.md, Pinned and floating): the tray pins, a pop-out floats. It matters because the bar is on every page and must still read when the group grows.

Refreshed 2026-11-06 · source: mock-ups from build/plan.py; not yet built

What it shows
  • Bar (N1): window lights, sidebar, the logo (Home), presence, Find, the notification indicator, speak to everyone, the time, pin, theme; 36 points
  • Presence (N6): up to three rigs as chips, each a shape and its name; more as one control, "10 rigs", the worst mark and its word ("1 gone"), opening the list
  • Notification indicator (N7): a bell, the worst mark (✓ nothing, ◆ look, ▲ urgent) and its words ("Nothing", "3 to look at", "4 · 1 urgent"); click for Home's Needs a look
  • Speak to everyone (N8): Mark all, Away… and Broadcast… as three icon buttons, each with its tooltip
  • Tooltips: on every control, words only, after a short pause or on keyboard focus; never an action
  • Right tray (T1) pins: it opens on a chosen thing, pushes the page, and stays until × or Escape
  • Pop-out (T2) floats: it opens from its control over the page, never pushes, and closes on Escape, a click outside, a choice, or its control again

Region codes: C, the context control (slate); N, navigation (teal); P, page content (brass); T, the tray and pop-outs (blue). Quote them as "0.5a P2".
