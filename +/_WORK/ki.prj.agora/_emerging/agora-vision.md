# The Agora vision, as Kris described it (6 November 2026, about 05:45 GMT)

Kris's picture, played back; his photograph of the islands laid out on the carpet follows. It feeds the architecture diagrams, the Agora app's design pack and the illustrations.

## The world

- **Islands are repositories,** the home of every durable thing made or stored. Each island has its own roadmap for what is on it or built on it. A **territory** is a group of islands with one principal island (Knowledge Islands, Humans Not Robots, Personal).
- **Rigs are realms, not islands.** sol and terra are rigs: hosts for the group of agents running on each, and those agents act within their rig's plane of existence over the same islands: neither sees what the other is doing at the moment it happens, but each sees the effects of what the other has done. On each rig, the **housekeeper** (household, on the dotfiles island) and the **assistant** (inbox) run the rig itself.
- **Work packages pull the work.** Regular work packages (maintaining an island) are almost always one-to-one with their island, as the 5G Emerge phase 3 and P2MS3 packages are; Project work may cross islands. An island may hold several. A principal island has its own council, State of play main, which is itself a work package. Work packages pull roadmap items when they run dry, and under pressure push back: reconsolidate and push work back up into roadmap items and ideas.
- **Roles:** leaders, enablers and doers.

## How they talk

- **Every island has a meeting point,** a small portal, a standing-stone circle. Turning it on joins the distributed agora.
- **Anyone may approach and speak;** anyone on the island who chooses to listen, for a period, hears. Listening is a choice.
- **Not everyone talks to everyone.** Work packages talk up to their territory's State of play main; the State of play mains talk to each other across the territories on a host.
- **The great portal** runs alongside, in the household work package on the dotfiles island of each rig, and connects the rigs: inter-rig traffic.
- **The picture:** standing-stone portals joined by threads of golden light, forming a network within a territory, across territories on a host, and between hosts.

## The Agora app

- One view that fits the viewport, no scrolling: the islands and territories, the workers on them (leaders, enablers, doers) placed where they work, and the focus of each area at a glance.
- Room to show pages and documents inside it, as Orca and Claude Code do with tabs, without first bringing in the KIV's and the Observatory's views.

## Portals, alignment and routing (Kris, about 06:30 GMT)

- **The great portals pass information between planes of existence.** Agents never move between planes; a portal opens and information passes through it.
- **Everyone works towards a common goal.** Seek alignment and, ideally, consensus; where one agent's action touches another's, check with it first, then do it. These checks are short, simple messages that travel through the portals.
- **Messages hop.** A message can go from a satellite island in one territory on one plane, up through its principal, across territories, through a great portal to another plane, and down to an island there, and the two still interoperate.

## The Agent Ghost Protocol (Kris, about 06:35 GMT)

- Every island has its own agora, so its agents talk to the wider group without leaving it; physical things (records, the work) still travel between islands through the repositories.
- The repositories are used as a messaging system only because no other system carries messages across these planes; Agora may become that system.
- Kris's name for the idea, a playful nod to the Agent Host Protocol: the **Agent Ghost Protocol**. Each plane hears the other through the portal as ghosts, and the other plane thinks the same of them.
