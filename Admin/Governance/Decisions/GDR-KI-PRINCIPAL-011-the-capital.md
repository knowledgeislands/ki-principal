# GDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-011: The Capital

## Context

[[SDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-002-the-home-of-knowledge|SDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-002]] and [The Home of Knowledge](https://github.com/knowledgeislands/ki-principal/blob/1e745753963b9dd1fa286e2d39d1ff8353233f9b/Pillars/Philosophy/Introduction/Background/The%20Home%20of%20Knowledge/The%20Home%20of%20Knowledge.md) place the Capital at the heart of every island. The Capital contains two buildings: the Library, the canonical record, and the Council Hall, where decisions are made. [[GDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-010-the-port|GDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-010]] gave the Council Hall the meaning of governance.

The `ki-design` work had drawn the civic heart as a walled Citadel and given the local app, the Observatory, a third building there: the Tower. A first record of those names was withdrawn on the same day while the naming was reopened, because a Tower and a Citadel read as dictatorial and fortified beside the friendlier Library and Council Hall. Visual Style also listed "citadels" as the terrain metaphor for governance.

On 2026-10-18 Kris decided: "I think it's capital... Let's stay with something which isn't considered to be Combative." Under [[SDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-004-the-enactment-process|SDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-004]] his explicit instruction for this bounded change is the ratification.

## Decision

**The Capital is the civic heart of an island, and the only name for that idea.** It holds two buildings:

- **The Library** - canonical knowledge.
- **The Council Hall** - governance.

**The Capital is drawn civic and welcoming: a square, never a fortress.** Each tile set draws it in its own way - a civic quarter and square in a modern set, a hilltop forum or acropolis in an ancient one.

**The symbol language stays non-combative.** Knowledge Islands draws no citadels, towers, keeps or battlements. "Citadel" is retired as a Knowledge Islands term, and the Tower is dropped.

**The Observatory is the mark of the local app.** The Observatory already means insight, and it is the app's name.

**The curtain wall stays open.** Whether a territory's Principal repository is drawn with a wall around its knowledge base is not decided; any answer must sit within the non-combative principle.

## Consequences

- The Symbol Library and Visual Style list the Capital, and the governance row of the terrain language names the Council Hall in the Capital rather than citadels.
- The Observatory symbol keeps its meaning of insight and analytics and is also the local app's mark.
- `ki-design` and the KI Website retire their Citadel and Tower wording, draw the Capital as civic, and mark the Capital, the Library and the Council Hall as official.
- Roadmap records keep their Citadel and Tower wording as a historical record.
