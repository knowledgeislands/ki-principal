8.2 · Guilds, reworked

Kris's review of 8.1, which this page supersedes. The guilds and their 65 skills, read from each skills/<guild>/<skill>/SKILL.md in ki-agentic-harness, with a chosen skill read in the right tray. Three changes from 8.1: the guild shields sit in one bar that scrolls sideways, with All guilds frozen first so the way back never scrolls away; the skills are a two-level tree table whose columns are the SKILL.md front matter itself, under the same summary-filter bar as Roadmap and Jobs; and a skill opens in the tray, not the bottom pane, so Guilds uses the bottom pane nowhere. It matters because the front matter is what a person compares across skills (what kind, when it applies, what it leans on, how it is called, which runtimes), and a table lets them see it for every skill at once, while the shields keep 8.1's sense of a guild as a place. Until the guildmaster's catalogue (ki.reg.guilds) names the guilds, each harness folder stands in. The territory is the context control's. There is no brass action and no gold: nothing on this page waits on Kris.

Refreshed 2026-11-06 · source: mock-ups from build/plan.py; not yet built

What it shows
  • Shield bar (P2): All guilds, frozen, with all 65 skills; then each guild's shield (monogram on its own colour), name and count; it scrolls sideways under a fade on each side that hides shields, and the bar's right end holds two chevrons and how many shields lie before and after; choosing a shield keeps that guild and adds a Guild chip
  • Summary-filter bar (P3): tallies All skills; ki-kind (Governance, Process); ki-applicability (Baseline, Detected, Declaration-only, Invocation-only), each counting what the other filters keep; chips (the context's territory first, then the chosen guild); one Filter menu (runtimes, depends on, has references/, scripts/ or assets/, columns); words in a name or description; Group by Guild, ki-kind or None
  • Tree table (P4): a guild row (fold chevron, shield, name, count, its about line and its mix of kinds) and its skills beneath, indented on a branch line: name (monospace), description (first clause, one line), ki-kind and ki-applicability (each a mark and its word), ki-depends-on (the first, then +N), argument-hint (monospace, one line), runtimes (ki-supported-runtimes, or any); a long guild shows its first seven and "Show N more"; with the tray open, argument-hint and Runtimes step out and the last header reads +2
  • Right tray (T1), a skill chosen: Skill · its guild · its harness, its name; tabs (T2) SKILL.md, references/, scripts/ and assets/, each with how many files, an empty folder greyed; SKILL.md shows the front matter (T3) as keys and values, then the body drawn (T4); a folder tab lists its files with sizes and draws the chosen one; Open in a tab (page 6), Copy its path, Show in Roadmap
  • No bottom pane on this page

Region codes: P, page content (brass); T, the tray and pop-outs (blue). Quote them as "8.2a P2".
