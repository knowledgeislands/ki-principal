# Streams

## Overview

Streams are Knowledge Islands Principal's operational container for knowledge in motion. Finite forward work lives flat in [Roadmap](Roadmap/Roadmap.md), while recurring obligations are defined once as Activity notes in the configured collection, currently [Admin Activities](../Admin/Operations/Activities/Activities.md); when an opted-in housekeeping Activity falls due it creates a linked ordinary roadmap record, so Streams holds no separate housekeeping area. Durable knowledge belongs in `Admin/`, `Pillars/`, or `Resources/`, not in Streams.

---

## Roadmap

[Roadmap](Roadmap/Roadmap.md) contains the flat work records and [allocation ledger](Roadmap/_ISSUES.md). Each record carries its own horizon, status, priority, dependencies, and current delivery detail; it never moves between priority folders.

---

## Projects

[Projects](Projects/Projects.md) is the territory's Project registry: one note per finite Project, named by its slug, carrying its outcome, lead, target and `lifecycle`, with an Outcome section and Notes for ideas and context. An idea-stage Project is a `planned` one whose outcome is a decision its lead takes. Links point upwards: each work record names its Project, so a Project note lists no records and carries no health or update section; status lives in each record.

---

## Themes

[Themes](Themes/Themes.md) holds one theme page per long-lived theme, named by its slug, which its Projects, projectless records and Activities name. Each page gives a Direction section, saying why it is a theme, what matters, its direction and the guidance common to its work, and Notes for ideas and context; it lists neither its Projects nor their records or Activities, and carries no lifecycle, lead, dated review or status section.
