One view that fits the window with no scrolling (MSG-62774774, Kris's vision): the territories and their islands, the work packages and workers placed where they work, the primary focus of each, the meeting point on each island, the threads of talk up to each territory's council and across, and the great portal between the Macs. It matters because it is the page the app opens on, and it should show the whole distributed Agora as one place.

Refreshed 2026-11-06 · source: mock-ups from build/plan.py; not yet built

What it shows
  • Territories, each a faint boundary with its name
  • Islands (repositories), drawn as hexes
  • Meeting point on each island: on (lit) or off
  • Workers by role: council, enabler, doer; each with its primary focus and rig
  • Planes: sol, terra or both, since each rig is a plane over the same islands
  • Listening by shape: solid listening, hollow on open, slashed silent
  • Threads of talk: work packages up to their council, councils across
  • The great portal: sol and terra, open or not
  • At a glance: agents by host, what needs a look, what waits on Kris, the portal; the key; Show as a list

Region codes: P, page content (brass). Quote them as "1a P2".
