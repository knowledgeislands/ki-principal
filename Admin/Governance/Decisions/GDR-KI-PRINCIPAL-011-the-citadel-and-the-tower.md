# GDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-011: The Citadel and the Tower

## Context

[[SDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-002-the-home-of-knowledge|SDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-002]] and [[The Home of Knowledge]] place the Capital at the heart of every island, holding the Library, the canonical record, and the Council Hall, where decisions are made. [[GDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-010-the-port|GDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-010]] gave the Council Hall the meaning of governance. Canon had no name for the walled heart in which these buildings stand: Visual Style mentions citadels only in passing, as a terrain metaphor for governance, and no earlier Decision Record names either the Citadel or the Tower.

The `ki-design` work already uses the Citadel as the civic heart of a Capital, drawn across a 7-hex flower, and draws the local app, working name "the Observatory", as a third building of the Citadel: the Tower, a place to watch, see and guide. The Observatory symbol keeps its own meaning of insight and analytics, so the app needs a mark that is not the domed observatory.

On 2026-10-18 Kris instructed that this record confirm the names already in use and add the Tower. Under [[SDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-004-the-enactment-process|SDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-004]] his explicit instruction for this bounded change is the ratification.

## Decision

**The Citadel is the civic heart of a Capital.** It holds three buildings on one walled terrace around a central square:

- **The Library** - canonical knowledge.
- **The Council Hall** - governance.
- **The Tower** - watching and guiding.

The Citadel is always drawn across a 7-hex flower, either as one sprite spanning the flower or as separate buildings on its ring, and never as a single-hex building.

**The Tower is the mark of the local app**, the Observatory, whatever the app is finally called. Its lamp is the Beacon. The domed Observatory symbol keeps its meaning of insight and analytics and is never the app's mark.

## Consequences

- The Symbol Library and Visual Style list the Citadel and the Tower, and [[The Home of Knowledge]] places the Library and the Council Hall within the Citadel, alongside the Tower.
- The governance row of the Visual Style terrain language ("Council Halls / citadels") stands: the Council Hall, in the Citadel, is where governance happens.
- `ki-design` marks the Citadel and the Tower as canon.
- The product name of the local app stays open; renaming it does not change its mark.
