The [skill badge frame](skill-badge-frame.svg) is a 96 × 36 SVG. Add a short label or glyph to the empty `skill-content` group, centred at (48, 16). Its content safe area is x=21–75, y=8–24. Use a glyph about 14 SVG units high or a short label around 11 units high. For a wider label, use adjacent app text.

## Agora app marks

- **Agora Lantern** `agora-lantern.svg`, `agora-lantern-1024.png`. A small lantern with a carrying handle and a visible flame stands on a low hex island. The two pairs of amber rays echo the Beacon’s signal. Slate teal, sage and warm paper carry the palette from Agora’s illustrations. The PNG is opaque and square, ready to receive the system icon mask.
- **Agora Beacon** `agora-beacon-template.svg`, `agora-beacon-template-16.png`, `agora-beacon-template-32.png`. A small tower sends two rays. Its lamp is separated from the tower by a full pixel at the 16-pixel master size. Black artwork on transparent backgrounds provides the template mask. The two PNGs support 16-pixel display at 1× and 2×.
