# GDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-013: The Causeway and the Mill

## Context

The Symbol Library gave the Bridge the meaning "translation layer". The local app, the Observatory, also says Bridge for its Act command: governed change, declared, confirmed and receipted. One word with two meanings in one product confuses readers and drawings alike.

[[GDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-010-the-port|GDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-010]] moved the meaning "operational systems" from the Port to `Admin/Operations` and left its drawn form open. The `ki-design` work proposed two names for that structure, the Mill and the Engine house.

On 2026-10-19 Kris accepted the `ki-design` recommendations on both questions. Under [[SDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-004-the-enactment-process|SDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-004]] his explicit instruction for this bounded change is the ratification.

## Decision

**The Causeway is the translation layer.** A causeway joins two grounds across shallow water, so that what crosses keeps its footing on both sides. It replaces the Bridge as a symbol.

**"Bridge" is freed for the local app's Act command:** governed change, declared, confirmed and receipted. Knowledge Islands no longer uses the Bridge as a symbol, and the bridge is never drawn for the Act command.

**The Mill is the structure for operational systems: the running of the island, its routines and upkeep.** It belongs to `Admin/Operations` and is drawn as a stone house with a waterwheel. The Engine house is not adopted.

Both are official.

## Consequences

- The Symbol Library and Visual Style list the Causeway as the translation layer and add the Mill.
- [[GDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-010-the-port|GDR-KI-PRINCIPAL-010]] no longer leaves the drawn form of `Admin/Operations` open.
- `ki-design` and the KI Website rename their translation symbol to the Causeway, use "Bridge" only for the Act command, and mark the Causeway and the Mill as official.
- Earlier Decision Records and roadmap records keep their Bridge wording as a historical record; read the Bridge as translation in them as the Causeway under this record.
