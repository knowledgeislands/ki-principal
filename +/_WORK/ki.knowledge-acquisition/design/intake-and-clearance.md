## Intake retention and pruning

The current contract retains closed receipts. Closing a request removes it from pending discovery but does not delete its receipt. Fourteen receipts currently exist: twelve closed, one blocked and one received/routing-pending; the directory including its README is 75,513 bytes. There is no implemented or agreed receipt-pruning operation, and a work-folder tidy does not apply to the separate intake receipt store.

Recommendation for a bounded design: keep one compact receipt per request with identity, provenance, bounded scope, preserved outputs, hashes, preservation/handling revisions, disposition and unresolved limits. Remove redundant progress commentary only when its source and authority evidence are safely homed; a receipt containing the only supplied-message bytes must retain that evidence. This is a proposed compaction rule, not permission to remove receipts now. Avoid a second archive, pending list or per-capture work queue.

Any later deletion of a full receipt needs an explicit surviving-proof contract first. Source identities, retry relationships, handled-source suppression and recovery after output dispatch must remain verifiable. Receipt closure proves the submitted scope was preserved; it does not prove receiver reconciliation or supply general source-retirement authority. A receipt age alone supplies no deletion authority.
